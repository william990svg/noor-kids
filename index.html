📖 Stories with a Lesson

Tap a story to read it. Every story teaches something good!

Once, a man was walking in the hot desert. His throat was dry and he was very thirsty. He found a deep well, climbed down, and drank the cool water.

When he climbed back up, he saw a little dog panting in the heat. The dog was so thirsty that it was licking the wet sand. The man's heart felt soft.

"This dog is as thirsty as I was," he thought. So he climbed down the well again, filled his shoe with water, held it in his teeth, and climbed all the way back up. Then he gave the water to the dog.

Allah loved his kindness so much that He forgave the man's sins. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told us this story so we remember: kindness is never wasted.

💛 Moral: Be kind to every living thing — even a small act of kindness is big with Allah.

Ali was a poor woodcutter. Every day he cut wood in the forest and sold it to buy food for his family. One day, his old axe slipped from his hands and fell deep into the river.

"Oh no!" cried Ali. "That was my only axe. How will I feed my family now?" He sat by the river and prayed to Allah for help.

Suddenly, an angel appeared from the river holding a shiny golden axe. "Is this your axe?" asked the angel. "No," said Ali. "Mine was old and made of iron."

The angel came back with a silver axe. "Is this yours?" "No," said Ali again. Then the angel brought Ali's old iron axe. "Yes! That is mine!" said Ali happily.

The angel smiled. "Because you were honest, you may keep all three axes." Ali went home with a happy heart — and a big lesson.

💛 Moral: Honesty is the best treasure. Allah always rewards the truthful.

Sara planted a tiny seed in a small pot. She watered it every morning and put it in the sunshine. But day after day, nothing grew.

"Why is it taking so long?" Sara asked her mother. Her mother smiled. "Good things take time, my love. Keep watering it, and be patient."

Sara did not give up. She watered the seed every day and made dua for it. One morning, she saw a tiny green sprout! A few weeks later, it had grown into a beautiful plant with bright flowers.

Sara learned that patience makes beautiful things grow — just like Allah grows our good deeds, little by little, every day.

💛 Moral: Be patient (Sabr). Small good deeds, done every day, grow into something beautiful.

Stories are told in words only — we never draw pictures of the Prophets. 🤲

🤲 Daily Duas — Learn & Say Them!

Small duas for every day. Tap 🔊 to hear each one.

Before Eating
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَعَلَى بَرَكَةِ اللَّهِ
Bismillahi wa 'ala barakatillah
"In the name of Allah, and with the blessings of Allah."
After Eating
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا
Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amana wa saqana
"All praise is for Allah, who fed us and gave us drink."
Before Sleeping
بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya
"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."
For Parents
رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا
Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani sagheera
"My Lord, have mercy on them, as they raised me when I was small."
For Knowledge
رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
Rabbi zidni 'ilma
"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."
